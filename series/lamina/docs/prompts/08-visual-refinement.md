# Visual refinement of the science volumes

## User request (verbatim)

> the quality of the first 3 books made with claude opus 5 is far superior than the last 3 made with codex gpt-6 astra in this session, can you improve them, for example, the father and daugther story looks squared, non realistic and boring, improve it for all the last 3

The attached reference showed the beach plate: rectangular torsos, simple limb
cylinders and sphere heads, a rectangular sand platform and sky wall, repeated
wave lines, and a solid orange flame. It establishes the shortcomings being
revised, rather than the desired finish.

## Art direction

Use the original snake, flower and skull volumes as the local benchmark for
subject-specific geometry, material variation, readable structure and movement.
The science volumes retain the same books, bilingual text, author marks,
left-page narration and inspection controls.

- **Campfire:** sculpted human anatomy and expressive seated poses; a father and
  daughter oriented towards one another and the fire; irregular coastal relief,
  wave surfaces, textured wood, layered flame and coherent atmospheric motion.
  Carry the same material quality into the five explanatory plates.
- **Cells:** organic cutaways and folded membranes, differentiated organelles,
  molecular surface detail and volumetric division. Preserve explanatory colour
  and clearly state the model's illustrative scope.
- **Atoms:** composed scientific plates with depth and material context. Preserve
  probability interpretation, isotope counts, water geometry, carbon connectivity
  and the difference between an energy diagram and physical space.

Review the actual reader at normal size and close up, paused at multiple moments,
with layers separated and reassembled. A successful contract test alone is not a
visual-quality verdict. New visual details must agree with the text and sources
in both languages. Keep the original three volumes unchanged.

## Revision note printed in the books

The science illustrations were rebuilt with sculpted organic forms, richer material detail and more expressive motion after a visual comparison with the original volumes. The author’s refinement request and art direction are recorded in docs/prompts/08-visual-refinement.md.
